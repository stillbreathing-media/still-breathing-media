Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Still Breathing Media ("we," "us," "our") creates faith-based stories, journals, and resources intended to bring hope and encouragement. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Sanctuary Wall submissions: if you share a prayer request or burden through our Sanctuary Wall, we collect what you write. You may submit anonymously — we do not require your name or contact details.
  • Purchases: if you buy a journal, book, or resource, payment is processed by our third-party payment provider (currently Gumroad). We do not directly collect or store your card details — that information is handled entirely by the payment provider under their own privacy policy.
  • Email address: if you choose to sign up for updates or a devotional series, we collect your email solely to send you that content.

How We Use It

  • To read, and where appropriate pray over, submissions to the Sanctuary Wall.
  • To fulfill purchases and deliver digital products.
  • To send occasional updates about new stories, resources, or ways to support the mission — only to those who've opted in.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Anonymous Submissions

The Sanctuary Wall is designed to be a safe space. Submissions are reviewed by our team before anything is shared publicly, and identifying details are never published without explicit permission.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party platforms — including Gumroad for payments and standard web hosting infrastructure — to run this site. These providers may collect limited technical data (such as IP address or browser type) as part of standard security and fraud prevention. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Your Choices

  • You can unsubscribe from any email list at any time via the link in that email.
  • You can request that we delete a Sanctuary Wall submission by contacting us with enough detail to identify it.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to hello@stillbreathingmedia.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site grows. Significant changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date above.
