Who we are

Still Breathing began as a channel.
It's becoming a lifeline.

We tell stories about the moments faith meets real life — doubt, grief, silence, and the quiet grace that shows up anyway. But a story is only the beginning of what we're building.

Still Breathing Media exists for one reason: to reach people who feel like they've run out of hope. The ones who feel forgotten. The ones carrying something they've never said out loud. The ones who wonder, quietly, if their story is already over.

It isn't. That's the whole message, in five words: if you're still breathing, your story isn't over.

Every film, every book, every free prayer on this site is a small piece of a larger goal — turning this platform into a real support network. Not just content to watch, but a place people can actually be met: a Sanctuary Wall to lay down what they're carrying, resources built to help people rebuild, and eventually, real outreach — counselors, mentors, and people on the ground for those who need more than a video can give.

What we believe

Pain isn't the end of the story

Every story we tell insists on this, even when it takes the whole runtime to get there.

Doubt is allowed in the room

We don't skip past hard questions about faith. We sit with them, honestly.

Nobody should carry it alone

The Sanctuary Wall, our prayers, and everything else here exist so no one has to.
