Who we are

Still Breathing began as a channel.
It's becoming a lifeline.

Somewhere tonight, someone is scrolling at 3am, convinced their story is already over. That's who this exists for. Not the version of them everyone else sees — the version underneath, the one carrying something they haven't said out loud.

Still Breathing Media makes cinematic stories about the moments faith meets real life — doubt, grief, silence, and the quiet grace that shows up anyway. But we didn't build this to be watched and forgotten. We built it because a film ends in two minutes, and the person watching it is still awake, still hurting, long after the credits roll.

So we kept building. Free prayers for the specific hours and specific weights people actually carry — not generic comfort, but words for 3am, for job loss, for the ones who feel forgotten. A Sanctuary Wall where what's too heavy to say out loud can be said anonymously, and actually read, actually prayed over. Books that go further than a film can, telling the fuller story of what it costs to keep going, and what's on the other side of it.

None of this is the finished thing. It's the beginning of one. The goal is a real support network — mentors, counselors, people on the ground — reachable by anyone who needs more than a video, wherever they are, however they find us. If you're still breathing, your story isn't over. That's not a slogan we came up with to sound hopeful. It's the whole reason this exists.

This is what we're building toward — not just stories on a screen, but real hands reaching real people.

What we believe

Pain isn't the end of the story

Every story we tell insists on this, even when it takes the whole runtime to get there.

Doubt is allowed in the room

We don't skip past hard questions about faith. We sit with them, honestly.

Nobody should carry it alone

The Sanctuary Wall, our prayers, and everything else here exist so no one has to.
