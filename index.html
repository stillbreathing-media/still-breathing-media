Prayers, always free

Whatever hour you're reading this, whatever you're carrying — these are yours, no cost, no login.

The 3:00 AM Prayer

Lord, You know why I'm awake. The night feels longer when the world is asleep and I'm still turning this over. I'm not asking You to explain it right now — just to sit with me in it. Quiet my mind enough to rest. Remind me that morning has never once failed to come. Amen.

For Lost Hope

God, I've run out of the version of hope that feels like excitement. What I have left is smaller and quieter — just enough to keep breathing. Meet me there. Don't ask me for more than I have today. Grow what's left, even if I can't feel it growing. Amen.

For a Job, Provision

Father, You know what's owed and what's running low. I'm not asking for more than I need — I'm asking for a door. Open the one that's mine. Give me strength to keep showing up while I wait, and keep worry from being louder than trust. Amen.

For a Loved One

Lord, I bring [name] before You — You know what I can't fix and what I can't stop worrying about. Cover them where I can't reach. Give them what they need, even if it isn't what I would have chosen for them. Let them feel less alone tonight than I sometimes feel able to make them. Amen.

For Depression, Heaviness

God, some days everything feels like more weight than I can carry, including this prayer. I'm not asking to feel better right away. I'm asking You to stay close while I don't. Remind me that this heaviness is not the whole truth about me or my life. Amen.

For Feeling Forgotten

Lord, it feels like everyone moved on and I got left in the part of the story nobody's reading anymore. Remind me You haven't looked away. I don't need to be first in line for everyone else — I need to know I'm not invisible to You. Amen.

Casting Off the Enemy

In the name of Jesus, I refuse every lie trying to convince me I'm too far gone, too tired, too broken to matter. Fear, shame, condemnation — you don't get the final word here. I belong to God, and I'm still standing, still breathing, still His. Amen.
