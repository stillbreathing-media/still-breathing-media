Still Breathing started as a channel. It's becoming a lifeline.

Every short film we make comes from the same conviction: that pain isn't the end of the story, and that someone, somewhere, needs to hear that tonight. We tell those stories on screen — and we're building the place where people can bring their own.

Every view, every journal sold, and every gift given here goes toward one goal: turning this into a real support network for people who've lost hope — starting with the ones already writing to us.
